## Decision — keep costs minimal

Prefer an **Oregon Assumed Business Name (DBA)** of existing ABSOL LLC, if ABSOL LLC is active and eligible and name checks pass. Do **not** create a second LLC just to start the technology brand. The official Oregon fee schedule lists **$50** for new DBA registration and **$50** every two years for renewal; verify current figures at submission.

**State processing is not completed by this document.** Oregon Corporation Division decides name eligibility at filing. DBA registration does not create a separate legal entity, transfer intellectual property, establish trademarks, or replace a professional license.

- [ ] Prepare the **actual physical principal business address** and registrant address, an authorized representative, a suitable notice mailing address, business email, daytime phone, and intended Oregon counties. - [ ] In the official Oregon Business Registry, select **New Assumed Business Name** (NOT New LLC): https://sos.oregon.gov/business/Pages/obr-welcome.aspx .

- [ ] Review the state declaration and complete electronic signature and **$50** fee in the government system.
