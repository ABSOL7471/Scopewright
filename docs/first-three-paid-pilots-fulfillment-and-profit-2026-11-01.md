# Scopewright — first three paid pilots: intake, delivery gate, and contribution tracker

**Prepared:** 2026-11-01 · **Status:** INTERNAL / owner approval required before any customer use  
**Seller for a pilot:** existing **ABSOL LLC**. `ABSOL Technologies` is a proposed DBA, **not confirmed registered**. Scopewright is a proposed early **contractor-reviewed drafting service**, not validated unattended estimating software.

**Cash-first outcome:** collect payment from **three real, consenting contractor customers** and measure whether each scope job makes a positive contribution after actual direct costs and delivery time. A drafted email, demo, verbal interest, unpaid test, or promised invoice is **not** a paid pilot.

## A. Prospect-facing offer card (copy only after owner approval)

> **Scopewright: a contractor-reviewed scope draft from your rough job-walk notes.**  
> **Introductory pilot price to test:** $99 for one straightforward residential job; multi-area or unusual jobs quoted in advance (proposed $149–$199, not guaranteed).  
> **You provide:** de-identified/permissioned notes and decisions already known; no customer names, street addresses, access codes, or unneeded photos.  
> **You receive:** an editable draft separating proposed work, exclusions, selections/allowances, and unanswered questions, plus a printable version and **one** reasonable correction round.  
> **You remain responsible:** verify all facts, quantities, permits, costs, code and final contract terms before using or sending the document. This is **not** an estimate, engineering review, legal contract, or accuracy guarantee.  
> **Before we accept any real notes:** agree on the work scope, fee, payment timing, revisions, secure transfer, processing method and data-deletion date. There is no subscription requirement.

**Do not say:** "never hallucinates", "guaranteed code-compliant", "fully validated", "ABSOL Technologies is registered", "Scopewright is exclusively owned by ABSOL LLC", or "no data ever leaves your machine" for a done-for-you service. The local-first app and operator-provided service have different privacy implications.

## B. Three-minute qualification questions (no customer information required)

1. About how often do you write scopes from handwritten/phone walkthrough notes?
2. Is the job a **straightforward single-area residential scope**, or a complex, hazardous, structural, multi-trade or regulated project? For the first three pilots, prefer **straightforward low-risk jobs**.
3. Would you review and approve the draft before giving it to your client, and can you provide notes with permission and without identifying client details?
4. What matters most: saving writing time, catching omissions, clearer exclusions, or organizing allowance choices?
5. If a reviewed example looks useful, would you consider one **$99 paid test** with the fee, deliverable and revision policy agreed in advance?

Record willingness to pay as **interest only** until a payment is actually received. Do not pressure anyone or claim the price is validated.

## D. Operator delivery checklist — every single pilot

1. **Confirm legal seller and offer.** Invoice as `ABSOL LLC` until the DBA is accepted; do not represent unverified DBA or IP ownership as official. No charges without owner approval.
2. **Obtain consent and minimize data.** Verify the contractor is authorized to share de-identified job notes; establish actual processing and deletion arrangements.
3. **Record source notes unchanged** in private storage. Keep a copy for factual cross-checking during the agreed retention period.
4. **Generate a draft** with Scopewright on a verified, approved local setup; record model/version and date. If software isn't ready, do not imply it generated an editorial example.
5. **Line-by-line source check:** for every inclusion, measurement, brand, material, cost, schedule and work authorization, find support in the original notes or mark as **needs contractor confirmation**. Remove unsupported statements.
6. **Uncertainty gate:** every `TBD`, `?`, `verify`, `maybe`, `soft`, `unknown` or missing selection that affects scope must appear as a question or explicit unpriced exclusion. The schema validator alone cannot establish this.
7. **Risk gate:** no unsupported structural, hazardous, permit, engineering, insurance, or code-compliance assertions. Flag specialist review when necessary.
8. **Contractor approval gate:** mark the deliverable `DRAFT — CONTRACTOR REVIEW REQUIRED`. Contractor reviews every inclusion, exclusion, allowance and unknown before using it with a client.
9. **One correction round:** log what was changed, by whom, and actual minutes spent. New work scope may require a new quote.
10. **Complete payment and delivery records:** record invoice date, actual payment date, gross receipts, refunds, payment fees, direct costs, time, and follow-up interest. A paid pilot counts **only when actual money is received**.
11. **Honor the agreed deletion date** and record deletion in private customer records. Do not store client data in public GitHub or use a prospect's logo/testimonial without permission.

**Quality hold:** if an important unknown disappears or an unsupported fact survives review, do **not** release the draft; log the miss privately and use it to improve the test set.

**Formulas (do not populate with invented amounts):**

- **Net cash receipts** = cash collected − actual refunds.
- **Cash contribution** = net cash receipts − processing fees − direct out-of-pocket job costs. This is **not** net company profit.
- **Cash contribution per delivery hour** = cash contribution ÷ actual total delivery hours (if >0). This is **not** an employee wage or net profit; operator labor, taxes and fixed overhead still matter.
- **Labor-adjusted contribution** = cash contribution − (actual delivery hours × chosen internal labor cost/hour). Label the labor rate as an **assumption** unless paid wages are documented.
- **Paid pilot count** = number of unique contractors with at least one **received payment** for an agreed Scopewright pilot, excluding fully refunded/cancelled jobs. Do not double-count repeat jobs as new pilot customers.
