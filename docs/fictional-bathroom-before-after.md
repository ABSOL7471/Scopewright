# Scopewright pilot: before/after bathroom example

**Status:** illustrative, fictional, manually written sample. **Not** a verified Scopewright model output or real client proposal.

## BEFORE — raw job-walk notes

> Guest bath - tub stays. Pull loose tile surround + old drywall by tub, backing may need work once open. Re-tile surround; owner picks tile later. Vanity maybe stays? Fan noisy, might need swap, vent route TBD. Floor at doorway feels soft - check when opened. Paint walls after dust control. Haul debris. Measure again next visit.

## AFTER — sample reviewable draft

### Proposed inclusions
1. Protect adjacent finishes and establish dust containment before selective demolition.
2. Remove loose tile and affected drywall at tub surround; collect and dispose of debris.
3. Inspect exposed backing and adjacent surfaces. Repairs beyond the agreed scope require review and authorization.
4. Prepare approved substrate and waterproofing details for the new tub surround; install selected tile after material and layout confirmation.
5. Prepare and paint bathroom walls within the agreed area after dusty work.

### Allowances / selections pending
- Tile selection, pattern, trim and quantities are **not yet confirmed**. No brand, dollar allowance or dimensions are assumed.

### Open questions
- Is the vanity staying? Any removal/reinstallation requested?
- Is the bath fan replacement in scope? Which model? Where will ducting terminate?
- What is the condition of the soft floor by the doorway after inspection?
- What are the verified tile dimensions, termination points and paint limits?
- Who handles permits or licensed specialty work, if applicable?

### Exclusions unless separately authorized
- Structural and concealed water-damage repairs beyond initial inspection.
- Electrical alterations, new fan duct routing and panel work.
- Vanity replacement, flooring replacement and unrelated finishes.
- Unspecified material upgrades or changes after selection.

## Contractor review checklist
- [ ] Every included item is traceable to the original notes or explicitly labeled as a proposal for approval.
- [ ] No unsupported measurements, dollar amounts, material brands or repair promises.
- [ ] The uncertain vanity, fan and floor conditions remain questions.
- [ ] Permit, trade, code and waterproofing requirements are verified by the contractor.
- [ ] Customer selections and scope boundaries are agreed before pricing or signing.

## How to use this demo
Show a prospective contractor how Scopewright is *intended* to help structure information. **Do not claim** this exact example was generated or passed validation by the software. Before real customer delivery, run the local model on permissioned or fictional notes, verify against the original notes, and review every line.
