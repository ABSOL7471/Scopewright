## Five-question discovery call — target 7 minutes
1. **Current process:** “After a walkthrough, what do you use to turn rough notes into a client-ready written scope?”
2. **Pain:** “Which part takes the most time: inclusions, exclusions, unanswered selections, or formatting?”
3. **Fit:** “Would a contractor-reviewed draft help on a simple paint, trim, or cosmetic remodel job? Or would that be a poor fit?”
4. **Trust:** “What would you need to see before trusting a draft—side-by-side notes, clear unanswered questions, editable output, or something else?”
5. **Buying signal:** “If you like a fictional example, would you consider a one-time $99 pilot for a simple job after we agree on privacy, deliverables, and terms? If not, what price or condition would change that?”

**Record privately:** prospect, contact permission, date, top pain, trust blocker, fit (yes/no), next step, explicit price feedback, and permission to follow up. Do not store personal or customer details in public GitHub.

## Objection-handling crib sheet (not promises)
| Prospect says | Grounded answer | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| “I already use ChatGPT.” | “That makes sense. The service is about a repeatable, reviewable format with exclusions and open decisions, not claiming a better model. Would you compare a fictional example?” | No pressure; record feedback |
| “Will it price my job?” | “No. The contractor determines measurements, selections, pricing, permits, and final scope.” | Decline if automatic pricing is required |
| “Can I upload a client's photos?” | “Not yet. We'd first agree on permissions, a private transfer method, processing tools, retention, and deletion. Please don't send identifiable material now.” | Stop intake until terms are complete |
| “Can it handle deck structure or mold?” | “Not as an initial paid pilot. Those require qualified assessment; a draft cannot certify safety, code, or capacity.” | Redirect to simple job or decline |
| “Is this your software?” | “It's a Scopewright-assisted service offered by ABSOL LLC; commercial IP permissions and product claims are being reviewed.” | Do not claim exclusive ownership |
| “What if it's wrong?” | “The contractor must line-check and approve every draft. We'd agree on correction and refund terms before taking a payment.” | No unapproved guarantee |
| “Can I see proof?” | “I can show a clearly labeled fictional, human-authored illustration. We are still conducting real-job validation.” | Share only after owner approval |

## Qualification gate — do not take a job until all true
- [ ] Simple, nonstructural, nonhazardous residential scope; no engineering, permit certification, insurance adjustment, or legal contract promise
- [ ] Owner-approved price, exact deliverable format, turnaround, revision limit, cancellation/refund terms
- [ ] Verified seller/payment method under **ABSOL LLC**; ABSOL Technologies DBA is **not yet verified registered**
- [ ] Commercial software/IP authority reviewed; public MIT copyright notice is individual
- [ ] Written consent to process de-identified notes, with actual tools, secure transfer and retention/deletion documented
- [ ] Human review of notes against generated draft, with all unknowns clearly flagged
- [ ] Contractor accepts responsibility for final review, pricing, and customer authorization

If any gate is unmet: **discovery/demo only**, no paid intake or real customer notes.

## One-prospect conversion sequence (draft, not approved)
